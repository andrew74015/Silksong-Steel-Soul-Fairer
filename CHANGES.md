# What the save carries and what it resets

## Carried over

- Twenty-eight tools, unlocked, the ones that use ammunition already stocked
- Three crests: the upgraded Hunter, equipped, plus Spell and Toolmaster
- Swift Step and Cling Grip
- The charged needle slash and Silk Spear
- The Hunter's Journal, map markers and every pin type
- All twenty-seven fleas already saved

## Reset

- Five masks
- One spool of silk, no regeneration, still broken as at the start of the game
- A base needle, and neither tool upgrade track started
- No rosaries and no shell shards
- No maps, no quill, no benches, no fast travel
- No bosses defeated, no quests started, an empty bestiary
- The play timer at zero

You begin in the opening room with nothing in the world touched. Faydown Cloak,
Clawline, Silk Soar, Drifter's Cloak and Needolin are all still out there to find.
